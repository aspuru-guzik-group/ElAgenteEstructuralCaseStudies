================================================================================
MECHANISTIC REPORT: 1,2,3-THIADIAZOLE S-OXIDE TO PYRAZOLE TRANSFORMATION
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
The transformation involves conversion of N-methyl-5-methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole-
1-oxide (N-Me TDSO) to N-methylpyrazole via sulfur monoxide (SO) extrusion.

SUBSTITUENT SPECIFICATIONS (from TS.png):
- Ring carbon alpha to sulfur (C5): Me (methyl)
- All other peripheral ring substituents: H
- N-alkyl group: Me (methyl)

TAUTOMERIC EQUILIBRIUM & DUAL PATHWAYS
--------------------------------------
The precursor NH-TDSO exists as two tautomers before N-alkylation:
- Tautomer 1: N-H on N2 (nitrogen adjacent to sulfur)
- Tautomer 2: N-H on N3 (nitrogen farther from sulfur)

N-alkylation with methyl fixes the position, generating two distinct substrates.

================================================================================
PATHWAY A
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 2-Methyl-5-methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole-1-oxide
- Structure: 5-membered ring with S=O
- N-Me position: N2 (directly bonded to sulfur)
- C-Me position: C5 (alpha to sulfur)
- Ring: S(=O)-N(Me)-N=C-C(Me)

TRANSFORMATION:
- SO extrusion via retro-[3+2] cycloaddition
- The N2-methyl group becomes N1-Me in the pyrazole product
- C5-methyl becomes C3-Me in pyrazole due to ring contraction/rearrangement

PRODUCT: 1,3-Dimethylpyrazole
- Structure: 5-membered pyrazole ring
- N-Me on N1
- C-Me on C3 (β to N-Me)
- Ring: N(Me)-N=C-C-C(Me)

File: pathway_A_substrate.xyz → pathway_A_product.xyz

================================================================================
PATHWAY B
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 3-Methyl-5-methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole-1-oxide
- Structure: 5-membered ring with S=O
- N-Me position: N3 (farther from sulfur)
- C-Me position: C5 (alpha to sulfur)
- Ring: S(=O)-N=N(Me)-C-C(Me)

TRANSFORMATION:
- SO extrusion via retro-[3+2] cycloaddition
- The N3-methyl group becomes N1-Me in the pyrazole product
- C5-methyl becomes C5-Me in pyrazole (adjacent to N-Me)

PRODUCT: 1,5-Dimethylpyrazole
- Structure: 5-membered pyrazole ring
- N-Me on N1
- C-Me on C5 (α to N-Me)
- Ring: N(Me)-N=C-C-C(Me)

File: pathway_B_substrate.xyz → pathway_B_product.xyz

================================================================================
KEY MECHANISTIC FEATURES
================================================================================

1. SO EXTRUSION: The S=O unit leaves as sulfur monoxide (SO), reducing the
   ring from 5 atoms (S-N-N-C-C) to a transient 4-atom species.

2. RING REARRANGEMENT: The remaining N-N-C-C framework incorporates the
   α-methyl substituent to form the 5-membered pyrazole ring.

3. REGIOSELECTIVITY: The two pathways produce regioisomeric pyrazole products:
   - Pathway A: 1,3-dimethylpyrazole (methyl groups separated by 2 carbons)
   - Pathway B: 1,5-dimethylpyrazole (methyl groups in 1,2-relationship)

4. COMPOSITIONALLY IDENTICAL SUBSTRATES: Both pathway A and B substrates have
   the same molecular formula (C4H6N2OS) but differ in N-methylation site.
