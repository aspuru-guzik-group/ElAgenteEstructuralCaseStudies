================================================================================
MECHANISTIC REPORT: NH-TDSO to N-Alkyl Pyrazole Transformation
================================================================================

REACTION OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transformation: NH-1,2,3-Thiadiazole 1-oxide (TDSO) → N-Alkyl Pyrazole
Mechanism: Extrusion of sulfur monoxide (SO) with concurrent/sequential N-alkylation
Substituent Pattern:
  • α-carbon to S (position 5): Methyl (Me)
  • Other peripheral ring positions: Hydrogen (H)
  • N-alkyl group: Methyl (Me)

TAUTOMERIC EQUILIBRIUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The NH proton in the TDSO starting material can equilibrate between the two 
ring nitrogens, generating two neutral, compositionally identical tautomers:

  Tautomer 2H: H on N2 (nitrogen adjacent to sulfur)
  Tautomer 3H: H on N3 (nitrogen not adjacent to sulfur)

This tautomerism leads to two distinct reaction pathways producing regioisomeric
N-methylpyrazole products.

================================================================================
PATHWAY A
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 5-Methyl-2H-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
  • Structure: 5-membered ring containing S(=O), two N atoms, and two C atoms
  • Ring connectivity: S(1)-N(2,H)-N(3)=C(4)-C(5,Me)
  • Key feature: NH on N2 (adjacent to sulfur)
  • File: ts-study/structures/pathway_A_substrate.xyz
  • Molecular formula: C3H4N2OS

TRANSFORMATION:
  1. N-alkylation occurs at N2 (the nitrogen bearing H)
  2. Extrusion of sulfur monoxide (SO) from the ring
  3. Ring contraction/rearrangement to form pyrazole

PRODUCT: 1,3-Dimethylpyrazole
  • Structure: 5-membered pyrazole ring with N-Me and C-Me substituents
  • Ring connectivity: N(1,Me)-N(2)=C(3,Me)-C(4)-C(5)
  • Key feature: C-methyl at position 3 (adjacent to N2, the unalkylated nitrogen)
  • File: ts-study/structures/pathway_A_product.xyz
  • Molecular formula: C5H8N2

================================================================================
PATHWAY B
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 5-Methyl-3H-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
  • Structure: 5-membered ring containing S(=O), two N atoms, and two C atoms
  • Ring connectivity: S(1)-N(2)=N(3,H)-C(4)-C(5,Me)
  • Key feature: NH on N3 (not adjacent to sulfur)
  • File: ts-study/structures/pathway_B_substrate.xyz
  • Molecular formula: C3H4N2OS

TRANSFORMATION:
  1. N-alkylation occurs at N3 (the nitrogen bearing H)
  2. Extrusion of sulfur monoxide (SO) from the ring
  3. Ring contraction/rearrangement to form pyrazole

PRODUCT: 1,5-Dimethylpyrazole
  • Structure: 5-membered pyrazole ring with N-Me and C-Me substituents
  • Ring connectivity: N(1,Me)-N(2)=C(3)-C(4)-C(5,Me)
  • Key feature: C-methyl at position 5 (adjacent to N1, the alkylated nitrogen)
  • File: ts-study/structures/pathway_B_product.xyz
  • Molecular formula: C5H8N2

================================================================================
STRUCTURAL SUMMARY
================================================================================

                    Pathway A                    Pathway B
                    ---------                    ---------
SUBSTRATE:          2H-TDSO (H on N2)           3H-TDSO (H on N3)
                    NH adjacent to S             NH not adjacent to S

PRODUCT:            1,3-Dimethylpyrazole        1,5-Dimethylpyrazole
                    C-Me adjacent to N2          C-Me adjacent to N1
                    (opposite to N-Me)           (same as N-Me)

REGIOCHEMISTRY:     The position of the N-H in the TDSO tautomer determines
                    which nitrogen becomes N-methylated, thereby controlling
                    the relative positions of N-Me and C-Me in the pyrazole
                    product.
