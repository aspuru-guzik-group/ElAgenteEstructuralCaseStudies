================================================================================
                    MECHANISTIC REPORT: TDSO → PYRAZOLE TRANSFORMATION
================================================================================

REACTION OVERVIEW
-----------------
Transformation: 1,2,3-Thiadiazole S-Oxide (TDSO) → N-Methyl Pyrazole
Mechanism: Cheletropic SO extrusion via concerted transition state

SUBSTITUENT ASSIGNMENT
----------------------
• Ring carbon alpha to sulfur: -CH₃ (methyl)
• All other peripheral ring substituents: -H
• N-alkyl group: -CH₃ (methyl)

================================================================================
                           TAUTOMERIC EQUILIBRIUM
================================================================================

Before N-alkylation, the NH-TDSO exists in equilibrium between two tautomers:

  Tautomer A: N-H on nitrogen adjacent to sulfur (N2 position)
  Tautomer B: N-H on nitrogen remote from sulfur (N3 position)

N-methylation of each tautomer generates compositionally identical but 
regioisomeric substrates, leading to distinct reaction pathways.

================================================================================
                              PATHWAY A
================================================================================

Substrate: 2,5-Dimethyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
- N-methyl group on N2 (nitrogen adjacent to S)
- Methyl substituent on C5 (carbon alpha to sulfur)
- Molecular formula: C₄H₆N₂OS
- Structure file: pathway_A_substrate.xyz

Transformation:
- SO extrusion occurs via cheletropic elimination
- The S-N2 and S-C5 bonds cleave simultaneously
- A new C-C bond forms as the pyrazole ring contracts

Product: 1,4-Dimethylpyrazole
- N-methyl at position 1
- C-methyl at position 4 (two bonds from N-Me via carbon chain)
- Molecular formula: C₅H₈N₂
- Structure file: pathway_A_product.xyz

================================================================================
                              PATHWAY B
================================================================================

Substrate: 3,5-Dimethyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
- N-methyl group on N3 (nitrogen remote from S)
- Methyl substituent on C5 (carbon alpha to sulfur)
- Molecular formula: C₄H₆N₂OS
- Structure file: pathway_B_substrate.xyz

Transformation:
- SO extrusion proceeds by the same cheletropic mechanism
- Different N-methylation site leads to different ring closure topology

Product: 1,3-Dimethylpyrazole
- N-methyl at position 1
- C-methyl at position 3 (adjacent to N-N bond via one carbon)
- Molecular formula: C₅H₈N₂
- Structure file: pathway_B_product.xyz

================================================================================
                         KEY MECHANISTIC FEATURES
================================================================================

1. Regioselectivity Origin:
   The position of N-methylation determines the regiochemistry of the 
   pyrazole product. When N-Me is on the nitrogen adjacent to S (Pathway A),
   the carbon originally alpha to S becomes position 4 in the pyrazole.
   When N-Me is on the remote nitrogen (Pathway B), that same carbon 
   becomes position 3.

2. Stereochemical Considerations:
   The SO extrusion is typically suprafacial, proceeding through a 
   planar transition state. Both pathways involve concerted bond 
   reorganization.

3. Driving Force:
   Release of thermodynamically stable SO (sulfur monoxide) provides 
   the energetic driving force for this transformation.
