================================================================================
               MECHANISTIC REPORT: NH-TDSO to N-Alkyl Pyrazole
================================================================================

TRANSFORMATION OVERVIEW
-----------------------
N-Methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide → N-Methylpyrazole + SO (sulfur monoxide)

This is a cheletropic extrusion reaction where sulfur monoxide (SO) is expelled
from the five-membered 1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide ring, forming a pyrazole product.

SUBSTITUENT SPECIFICATIONS (per task requirements)
-------------------------------------------------
• Ring carbon alpha to sulfur (C5): Methyl (Me)
• All other peripheral ring substituents: Hydrogen (H)
• N-alkyl group: Methyl (Me)

TAUTOMERIC EQUILIBRATION
------------------------
Before N-alkylation, the parent NH-TDSO can exist in two tautomeric forms:
• 2H-tautomer: N-H on nitrogen adjacent to sulfur (N2)
• 3H-tautomer: N-H on nitrogen NOT adjacent to sulfur (N3)

N-methylation of each tautomer gives compositionally identical but 
regioisomeric N-methyl substrates, leading to two distinct reaction pathways.

================================================================================
                              PATHWAY A
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 2-Methyl-5-methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
  • N-methyl on N2 (adjacent to sulfur)
  • Ring methyl on C5 (alpha to sulfur)
  • Formula: C4H6N2OS
  • File: pathway_A_substrate.xyz

MECHANISM:
  The N-methyl group is positioned on the nitrogen adjacent to the sulfoxide.
  During SO extrusion, the C-S and N-S bonds break concertedly via a
  cheletropic transition state, and a new C-N bond forms.

PRODUCT: 1,3-Dimethyl-1H-pyrazole
  • N-methyl on N1
  • Ring methyl on C3 (same side of N-N bond as N-methyl)
  • Formula: C5H8N2
  • File: pathway_A_product.xyz

================================================================================
                              PATHWAY B
================================================================================

SUBSTRATE: 3-Methyl-5-methyl-1,2,3-thiadiazole 1-oxide
  • N-methyl on N3 (NOT adjacent to sulfur)
  • Ring methyl on C5 (alpha to sulfur)
  • Formula: C4H6N2OS
  • File: pathway_B_substrate.xyz

MECHANISM:
  The N-methyl group is positioned on the nitrogen NOT adjacent to sulfoxide.
  During SO extrusion, the same concerted mechanism operates, but the
  different N-methyl position leads to a different pyrazole regioisomer.

PRODUCT: 1,5-Dimethyl-1H-pyrazole
  • N-methyl on N1
  • Ring methyl on C5 (opposite side of N-N bond from N-methyl)
  • Formula: C5H8N2
  • File: pathway_B_product.xyz

================================================================================
                         KEY MECHANISTIC FEATURES
================================================================================

1. TAUTOMERIC CONTROL: The position of the exchangeable N-H before alkylation
   determines which nitrogen receives the N-methyl substituent.

2. REGIOCHEMICAL OUTCOME: The two compositionally identical N-methyl-TDSO
   substrates produce distinct pyrazole regioisomers (1,3- vs 1,5-dimethyl).

3. CONCERTED EXTRUSION: SO extrusion is a thermally-allowed cheletropic
   reaction that proceeds through a cyclic transition state.

4. BOND REORGANIZATION:
   • Bonds broken: C5-S, N-S (in both pathways)
   • Bond formed: C5-N (connecting to form pyrazole ring)
   • Expelled: SO (sulfur monoxide)
