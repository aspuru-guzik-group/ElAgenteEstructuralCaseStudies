# TS study: N-methyl TDSO -> N-methyl pyrazole

## Structural inference from `TS.png`
- Core transformation inferred: **concerted SO extrusion / ring contraction** of an **N-methyl 1,2,6-thiadiazine 1-oxide (TDSO)** to an **N-methyl pyrazole**.
- Requested substitution pattern applied:
  - ring carbon **alpha to sulfur** = **Me**
  - all other peripheral ring substituents = **H**
  - N-alkyl = **Me**
- Because the exchangeable NH can pre-equilibrate between the two ring nitrogens **before methylation**, two neutral regioisomeric N-methyl TDSO substrates were modeled:
  - **Pathway A substrate**: methyl on the ring nitrogen directly bound to S(O)
  - **Pathway B substrate**: methyl on the other ring nitrogen
- Under this minimal substitution pattern, the nominal pyrazole regioisomers collapse to the same constitutional product by 3/5 equivalence: **1,3-dimethylpyrazole**. To keep the reaction stoichiometrically balanced, the product structures include the coproduct **SO**.

## Method
- 3D seeds built from inferred connectivity and edited directly from the substrate geometries.
- Substrates optimized at **GFN2-xTB**.
- TS guesses generated by direct substrate editing toward simultaneous **C(alpha)-N bond formation** and **C(alpha)-S / N-S bond cleavage**.
- TS refinement: **Sella optTS + GFN2-xTB**.
- TS verification: **xTB Hessian/frequency analysis**.
- Product energy model: **singlet pyrazole + triplet SO** fragment sum, since isolated SO is triplet in its ground state.

## Frequency verification
- **Pathway A TS**: one imaginary frequency, **-429.52 cm^-1**
- **Pathway B TS**: one imaginary frequency, **-379.24 cm^-1**

## Mechanistic takeaways
- **Pathway A** originates from the lower-energy substrate.
- **Pathway B** starts **10.51 kcal/mol** higher than pathway A, but its TS is also lower on that local surface, giving a smaller local barrier (**9.90 kcal/mol** vs **22.87 kcal/mol** for A).
- On a common zero (pathway A substrate), **TS_B is 2.46 kcal/mol lower than TS_A**.
- Both pathways lead to the same stoichiometric product set for this substitution pattern: **1,3-dimethylpyrazole + SO**.
